# Drawings

The app icon and favicon are rendered from these by `make icons`.

The mark is the face of a Dwemer lexicon: a square plate bound at its corners, with a round dial whose center is the game's selection diamond. `lexicon-mark.svg` is the drawing the top bar uses, in the text color with the plate's face and the dial's inside cut out so the ground shows through (`web/src/lib/ui/Mark.svelte` carries the same path). `lexicon-icon.svg` is the same drawing in verdigris on the game's near-black in a rounded square, and `lexicon-icon-small.svg` a simpler one for the 16-pixel size, where the dial's ring and the plate's edges turn to mush: the corner brackets and the diamond alone.
